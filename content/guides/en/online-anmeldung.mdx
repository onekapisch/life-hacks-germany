## What online Anmeldung actually is

Germany's federal government has been rolling out **online residence registration (Wohnsitzanmeldung)** since 2022. Rather than attending a Bürgeramt appointment in person, you complete the process through the federal portal and authenticate yourself with an **eID (elektronischer Personalausweis)**.

It is not available everywhere, not for everyone, and not without technical requirements. Read this before assuming you can skip the in-person appointment.

---

## When you can use it

All three conditions must apply:

1. **Your municipality participates** — check at [wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de](https://wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de). Major cities that had rolled out participation by early 2026 include parts of Hamburg, Cologne, Stuttgart, and others. Munich's rollout has been slower. The list changes — verify before starting.

2. **You have a German ID card (Personalausweis) with activated eID function** — EU/EEA citizens with a German residence permit chip card may also qualify in some municipalities. Non-EU citizens with only a foreign passport cannot use the online process and must attend in person.

3. **You have the AusweisApp** — the official German government app (available on iOS and Android) that reads your ID card chip via NFC. Your smartphone must support NFC. Without it, you cannot authenticate.

---

## What you still need in every case

The online process does not eliminate document requirements — it digitises the submission:

- **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** — your landlord's signed confirmation that you have moved in (§19 BMG). Required online just as for an in-person appointment. Upload a scan or clear photo.
- **Your ID or passport details** — entered into the portal form
- **AusweisApp + NFC phone** — for identity verification

If you cannot produce the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung, neither the online nor the in-person process will work.

---

## Step-by-step: how the online process works

1. Go to [wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de](https://wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de) and select your municipality
2. Confirm the service is active for your city and your situation (new registration, not a change of name or marital status)
3. Start the form and enter your personal details and new address
4. Upload a scan of your Wohnungsgeberbestätigung
5. Authenticate your identity using AusweisApp: open the app, hold your ID card to the back of your phone, enter your eID PIN
6. Submit — you receive a digital **Meldebestätigung** (registration confirmation) by email or download

The whole process takes 10–20 minutes once you have the documents ready and the app configured.

---

## The eID PIN problem

Many people with German ID cards have never activated or set their eID PIN. If you do not know your PIN:

- **Request a new PIN letter** at [pin-brief.bundesdruckerei.de](https://www.pin-brief.bundesdruckerei.de) — the letter arrives in 2–4 weeks
- **Visit any Bürgeramt** with your ID card and ask them to set a new PIN on the spot — faster, no postal wait

If your eID chip is not activated (some older ID cards), you must go to the Bürgeramt. You cannot activate it remotely.

---

## When to use the online process vs. going in person

| Situation | Online | In person |
|---|---|---|
| German ID card + activated eID + participating city | ✓ | ✓ (either works) |
| Non-EU passport only | ✗ | ✓ |
| eID not activated | ✗ | ✓ |
| City not in the portal | ✗ | ✓ |
| WG sublet, non-standard address | Check city rules | Safer |

If there is any doubt about your eligibility, book an in-person appointment in parallel while you check. In high-demand cities, appointment slots book out weeks in advance.

---

## What you get at the end

The digital Meldebestätigung carries the same legal weight as a paper one issued at the Bürgeramt. It triggers the same downstream effects: BZSt sends your tax ID letter, you can use it as proof of address for banking and employer onboarding. Download it and save it in PDF — you will need it.

The Beitragsservice (Rundfunkbeitrag) will receive your registration data automatically, just as with an in-person registration.
